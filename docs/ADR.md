# Architecture Decision Records

## ADR-001: Modular Monolith
Accepted. Reduces operational complexity while preserving domain boundaries.

## ADR-002: PostgreSQL as Source of Truth
Accepted. Supports transactional accounting and mature tooling.

## ADR-003: AI Is Advisory
Accepted. AI cannot bypass deterministic strategy, risk, or execution controls.

## ADR-004: Paper Trading Before Sandbox
Accepted. Internal simulation must be validated before exchange test environments.

## ADR-005: Append-Only Portfolio Ledger
Accepted. Enables reconciliation and auditability.

## ADR-006: No Live Trading in MVP
Accepted. Reduces financial and security risk during validation.
