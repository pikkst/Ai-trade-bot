# The Daily Roast AI — Brand Guidelines

Last reviewed: 2026-07-31
Status: Authoritative brand specification

## 1. Official Brand

**Product name:** The Daily Roast AI

**Primary tagline:** Evidence-Driven Market Intelligence

**Supporting message:** Better decisions through evidence, not hype.

The product must be presented as an AI-assisted market intelligence, research, backtesting, and paper-trading platform. It must not be marketed as a guaranteed-profit trading bot.

## 2. Brand Purpose

The Daily Roast AI exists to help people understand markets through transparent evidence, reproducible analysis, deterministic risk controls, and clearly bounded AI assistance.

The word **Roast** represents critical analysis: the product challenges market narratives, identifies contradictions, highlights risk, and tests assumptions before a user acts.

## 3. Brand Promise

The Daily Roast AI does not promise profit. It promises:

- transparent evidence;
- explicit uncertainty;
- reproducible research;
- honest simulation;
- deterministic risk controls;
- complete decision lineage;
- human control.

## 4. Brand Positioning

The Daily Roast AI is positioned as an evidence-driven market intelligence platform.

It is not primarily:

- an exchange;
- a broker;
- a signal-selling service;
- a copy-trading platform;
- a meme-coin promotion channel;
- an autonomous live-trading system.

Its core product categories are:

- market research;
- explainable AI analysis;
- strategy comparison;
- risk intelligence;
- backtesting;
- paper trading;
- portfolio learning;
- audit and reporting.

## 5. Brand Personality

The brand is:

- intelligent;
- calm;
- analytical;
- critical;
- transparent;
- evidence-focused;
- technically credible;
- accessible without being simplistic.

The brand is not:

- sensational;
- aggressive;
- arrogant;
- manipulative;
- fear-driven;
- hype-driven;
- comedic at the expense of user trust;
- framed around rapid wealth.

## 6. Voice and Tone

### 6.1 General Voice

Use concise, clear, professional English. Prefer direct statements supported by evidence.

Good:

> Momentum strengthened over the last three finalized candles, but volatility and downside risk remain elevated.

Bad:

> BTC is about to explode. Buy now.

### 6.2 Analytical Tone

Analytical content must distinguish:

- observed facts;
- derived indicators;
- model interpretation;
- uncertainty;
- risk;
- action constraints.

### 6.3 UI Tone

UI text should be calm and operational.

Preferred:

- Analysis unavailable
- Market data is stale
- Risk policy rejected this action
- Paper portfolio halted
- Gemini quota exhausted; deterministic fallback used

Avoid:

- Oops!
- Huge opportunity!
- Moon incoming!
- Guaranteed edge

### 6.4 Marketing Tone

Marketing may be memorable, but it must remain factual.

Approved themes:

- Evidence over hype
- Research before risk
- Transparent AI
- Explainable market intelligence
- Practice before capital

Prohibited themes:

- guaranteed returns;
- effortless passive income;
- risk-free trading;
- secret winning algorithm;
- institutional profits for everyone;
- claims based only on backtests.

## 7. Naming Rules

### 7.1 User-Facing Name

Always use **The Daily Roast AI** on:

- website headings;
- application shell;
- authentication screens;
- reports;
- exports;
- documentation intended for users;
- release notes;
- marketing assets.

### 7.2 Acceptable Short Form

**Daily Roast AI** may be used in constrained UI areas after the full name has appeared.

### 7.3 Prohibited User-Facing Names

Do not use the following as the product name:

- AI Trade Bot;
- Crypto Bot;
- Trading Bot;
- Roast Bot;
- Daily Roast Trading Bot.

The GitHub repository may retain the technical name `Ai-trade-bot` until a separate repository-renaming decision is approved.

## 8. Product Descriptor

Use one of the following approved descriptors:

- Evidence-Driven Market Intelligence Platform
- AI-Assisted Market Research Platform
- Crypto Research and Paper-Trading Platform

The primary descriptor is:

> Evidence-Driven Market Intelligence Platform

## 9. Domain Strategy

### 9.1 Primary Domain

`thedailyroast.online`

### 9.2 Approved Subdomains

- `app.thedailyroast.online` — main application;
- `api.thedailyroast.online` — FastAPI backend;
- `docs.thedailyroast.online` — documentation;
- `status.thedailyroast.online` — future status page;
- `admin.thedailyroast.online` — future restricted administration interface.

The initial MVP may use:

- `thedailyroast.online` for the public landing page;
- `app.thedailyroast.online` for the application;
- `api.thedailyroast.online` for the backend.

## 10. Visual Identity Principles

The visual system should communicate intelligence, credibility, evidence, and controlled experimentation.

It should not resemble:

- a casino;
- a meme-token website;
- a high-pressure trading terminal;
- a get-rich-quick landing page.

## 11. Color Direction

The final color tokens belong in the design-system document. The approved direction is:

- deep navy or charcoal for primary surfaces;
- neutral slate tones for structure;
- warm amber as the distinctive Roast accent;
- green only for positive state, never as a profit promise;
- orange for caution;
- red for errors, halts, and material risk;
- blue for neutral informational states.

Color must never be the only carrier of meaning.

## 12. Typography Direction

Preferred product typography:

- primary sans-serif: Inter or another approved highly legible UI family;
- monospace: JetBrains Mono or an approved equivalent for identifiers, logs, and technical values;
- system fallbacks must be defined.

Typography must prioritize readability over novelty.

## 13. Logo Direction

The logo may combine concepts such as:

- a stylized roast or flame;
- a market chart;
- an analytical lens;
- a data signal;
- an abstract AI mark.

The logo must avoid:

- rockets;
- moons;
- sports cars;
- currency piles;
- guaranteed-upward charts;
- exchange-like visual imitation.

No final logo asset is approved by this document. Logo creation requires a separate reviewed design task.

## 14. Imagery and Illustration

Use:

- abstract data visualization;
- restrained market diagrams;
- research and intelligence motifs;
- evidence cards;
- analytical dashboards.

Avoid:

- luxury lifestyles;
- panic imagery;
- anonymous traders celebrating winnings;
- exaggerated green candles;
- misleading screenshots.

## 15. Product Language

Preferred terms:

- analysis;
- evidence;
- observation;
- market regime;
- risk;
- paper portfolio;
- simulated order;
- backtest;
- decision lineage;
- confidence;
- uncertainty;
- hypothesis.

Terms requiring explicit qualification:

- recommendation;
- signal;
- confidence;
- forecast;
- expected return;
- probability.

Never present AI confidence as probability of profit.

## 16. AI Persona

The AI is a research analyst, not a financial authority.

The AI must:

- cite supplied evidence;
- disclose contradictions;
- disclose missing information;
- state uncertainty;
- avoid urgency and emotional pressure;
- avoid unsupported claims;
- remain advisory.

The AI must not:

- guarantee outcomes;
- instruct users to ignore risk controls;
- frame simulation as live performance;
- claim access to information not supplied;
- conceal provider failure or data staleness.

## 17. Product Family

Future product modules may use the following naming pattern:

- The Daily Roast AI — Markets
- The Daily Roast AI — Research
- The Daily Roast AI — Portfolio
- The Daily Roast AI — Strategies
- The Daily Roast AI — Backtesting
- The Daily Roast AI — Paper Trading
- The Daily Roast AI — Alerts
- The Daily Roast AI — Labs
- The Daily Roast AI — Enterprise

Module names must be descriptive and must not imply regulatory status or guaranteed performance.

## 18. Legal and Trust Language

Every public surface that discusses trading or investment must clearly communicate:

- research and simulation purpose;
- no financial-advice status;
- no guarantee of profitability;
- cryptocurrency volatility;
- paper-trading status where applicable.

Disclaimers must be visible, readable, and not hidden behind misleading claims.

## 19. Accessibility and Inclusion

Brand implementation must:

- use sufficient contrast;
- avoid meaning conveyed only by color;
- support keyboard navigation;
- use plain language where possible;
- avoid culturally narrow jokes in critical workflows;
- make risk and simulation status unmistakable.

## 20. Brand Governance

Material changes to the following require documentation review:

- official product name;
- tagline;
- domain structure;
- logo;
- primary color system;
- product descriptors;
- financial or performance claims;
- AI persona;
- product family names.

Changes must update this document, README, product vision, UI guidance, marketing content, and relevant tasks.

## 21. Definition of Done for Brand Integration

Brand integration is complete when:

- user-facing documentation uses The Daily Roast AI;
- application title and metadata use The Daily Roast AI;
- domain references use `thedailyroast.online` and approved subdomains;
- UI distinguishes research, simulation, and risk states;
- marketing avoids prohibited claims;
- AI prompts and reports follow the approved analytical tone;
- README and documentation inventory include the brand documents;
- automated checks detect legacy user-facing product names where practical.
