# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Documentation
Product requirements, architecture, security, testing, APIs, data, AI, risk, paper trading, backtesting, and implementation tasks.

## Phase 1 — Research Core
Market ingestion, data quality, feature engineering, structured AI reports, and audit lineage.

## Phase 2 — Paper Trading
Strategy engine, risk engine, ledger, simulated execution, reconciliation, and 30-day EUR 20 experiment.

## Phase 3 — Backtesting and Dashboard
Reproducible backtests, benchmarks, reports, React dashboard, and observability.

## Phase 4 — Binance Sandbox
Restricted credentials, test environment, reconciliation, and operational runbooks.

## Phase 5 — Tiny Live Experiment
Only after explicit security and performance review. No withdrawals, strict limits, manual approval, immediate kill switch.

## Phase 6 — Productization
Multiple users and exchanges, stronger local LLM support, billing, compliance review, and SaaS controls.
